Fashion Affiliate Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fashion Affiliate Bot ("Fashion Affiliate Bot", "we", "us") handles information when it is used to prepare and publish fashion affiliate content to the operator's own connected TikTok account. Fashion Affiliate Bot is a personal content-automation tool operated by a single operator; it is not a public consumer service and does not collect data from TikTok viewers.

1. Who this applies to

Fashion Affiliate Bot is used only by its operator (the account owner). The only TikTok account connected to Fashion Affiliate Bot is the operator's own account. We do not access, request, or store data belonging to other TikTok users.

2. Information we access

When the operator authorizes Fashion Affiliate Bot with TikTok, Fashion Affiliate Bot may access the following through the TikTok API, strictly to provide its posting function:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic) — the operator's TikTok display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Content publishing access (scopes video.publish, video.upload, via the Content Posting API) — used only to upload media that the operator has explicitly approved.

Fashion Affiliate Bot does not read the operator's videos, followers, messages, or analytics, and never accesses any third party's TikTok data.

3. How we use information

  • To display which TikTok account is currently connected.
  • To upload and publish media that the operator has reviewed and approved.
  • To maintain a secure authorization session (OAuth access/refresh tokens) with TikTok.

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling, or resale, and we do not sell or share personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Data storage and security

Fashion Affiliate Bot stores only the operator's OAuth tokens and a local catalogue of the operator's own product images, captions and post history. Tokens are stored as protected environment variables on the hosting provider and are never displayed publicly. Access is limited to the operator.

5. Third-party services

To build content, Fashion Affiliate Bot uses a small number of service providers, each only for its stated function:

  • TikTok for Developers — publishing approved content to the operator's account.
  • OpenAI — generating captions from product information.
  • Google Drive — hosting the operator's own images used in posts.

These providers process only the data required to perform their function and under their own privacy terms.

6. Data retention

OAuth tokens are retained only while the account remains connected and are deleted when the operator disconnects the app or revokes access from their TikTok settings. Post history is retained locally for the operator's own reference and can be deleted at any time.

7. Your choices

The operator can revoke Fashion Affiliate Bot's access at any time from TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. Revoking access immediately stops all publishing and invalidates the stored tokens.

8. Children's privacy

Fashion Affiliate Bot is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under the age required by TikTok's own Terms of Service.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
